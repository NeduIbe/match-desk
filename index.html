          Betting words in plain English

          1X2
          1 = home team wins. X = draw. 2 = away team wins.
          Double chance (1X, X2, 12)
          Two results in one bet. 1X means the home team wins or draws.
          Draw no bet
          You pick a team. If the game is a draw, you get your money back.
          Over / Under 2.5 goals
          Over 2.5 means 3 or more goals in the game. Under 2.5 means 0, 1 or 2 goals.
          GG / NG
          GG means both teams score. NG means at least one team scores nothing.
          Handicap
          One team starts with a head start. "Home -1" means the home team must win by 2 or more goals.
          HT/FT
          The result at half-time and the result at full time. 1/1 means home leads at half-time and wins.
          Multigoal 2-3
          The total goals in the game will be 2 or 3.
          Clean sheet / Win to nil
          A team lets in no goals. Win to nil means they win and let in none.
          Fair odds
          What a bet should pay if our chance is right. Fair odds = 1 divided by the chance. If your bookmaker pays more than fair odds, that is a good price.
          Value
          On average, how much you win or lose for every 1 you stake. +5% is good. -5% is bad. It is only as good as our chance, so check the team news.

          Our numbers are estimates. They come from recent results and a simple goals formula. They are not bookmaker prices, and they do not know about injuries or lineups. If our number is very different from the bookmaker's, we are probably missing news.

          Checklist scores are rough weights from your match-day guide. They show which bets the conditions point to. They are not chances.

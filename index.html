          Which Nigerian-book markets this covers

          Model prices are estimates. They come from recent results, shrunk toward the league average, with a simple goals model. They are not bookmaker prices and ignore injuries, lineups and motivation until you add them. Bookmaker prices already absorb that news, so a big gap is more often missing information than free value.

          Rule scores are rough weights from the match-day guide. They rank how well conditions line up behind a market and are not probabilities.

          Bet responsibly. 18 and over only. Staking can lose money, and nothing here guarantees a result. Only stake what you can afford to lose.
